Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Do the checklists save progress?

No. This version keeps progress only while the page remains open and stores no personal data.

Can I print a checklist?

Yes. Use the Print checklist button; navigation, advertisements and controls are hidden in print.

Are the checklists suitable for every wedding?

They are adaptable starting points. Remove irrelevant tasks and add requirements from your family, officiant and vendors.

After the celebration
