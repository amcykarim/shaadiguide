Frequently asked questions
Does this builder use AI?
No. It combines your entries with predefined original templates in your browser.
Are my details saved or submitted?
No. This version does not submit or store the fields. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.
Can optional fields be blank?
Yes. Optional sections are omitted without placeholder punctuation. Review the draft aloud before using it.
