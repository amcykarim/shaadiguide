A limited directory of government starting points checked on 20 July 2026. It is not exhaustive and does not replace confirmation with the responsible local office.

General information, not legal advice

Requirements depend on the applicable law, state or union territory, district, jurisdiction and the couple's circumstances. Confirm the current process directly with the authorised Marriage Officer, registrar or official government portal before acting.

Verify before applying

Government URLs, services and jurisdiction rules can change. Verify on the government website before applying. If your state or union territory is not listed, search the National Government Services Portal or the official state website rather than using a commercial agent.
