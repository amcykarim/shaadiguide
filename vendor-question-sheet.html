Vendor Question Sheet

Printable interview sheet for recording wedding vendor answers, inclusions and follow-up questions.

  • Who will personally provide the service?
  • What is included and explicitly excluded?
  • What information, access or equipment do you need from us?
  • What are all fees, taxes, travel and overtime costs?
  • What happens after illness, delay, equipment failure or weather disruption?
  • What are the postponement, cancellation and refund terms?
  • When are deliverables or final services completed?
